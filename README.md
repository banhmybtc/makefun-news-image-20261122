The World in One Day — Sunday 22 November 2026. A program that writes one image summing up the world's news of 22 November 2026 to `/output/entry.png`.

The image is a newspaper front page drawn programmatically (see `draw22.py`, kept outside this repository): masthead, date, and six stories, all first reported on 22 November 2026 and verified against AP/AFP/Korea Herald and moneycontrol coverage:

1. **Climate — COP31 collapses** in Antalya; the EU and island nations reject a final text that never names fossil fuels.
2. **Gulf — two supertankers hit by mines** east of the Strait of Hormuz.
3. **Ukraine — Russian drone strike collapses a building** near Kyiv; at least 14 feared trapped.
4. **Sudan — drone attacks kill four near Khartoum** and damage a UN building.
5. **Mexico — cartel boss “El Chapo Isidro” captured** in Sinaloa; 11 died in the operation.
6. **Indonesia — 24 killed in a factory fire**; dozens still missing.

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
